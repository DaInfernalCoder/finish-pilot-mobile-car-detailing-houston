Interior Car Detailing

Interior car detailing focuses on the cabin: visible debris, carpets, floor mats, seats, footwells, crevices, cargo areas, and suitable high-use surfaces.

Menu evidence: Auto interior vacuuming, Steam cleaning, Seat shampooing, Interior scenting.

Exterior Car Detailing

Exterior car detailing begins with the visible outside surfaces of the vehicle and may combine a full-body wash with wheel washing and other documented appearance-care add-ons.

Menu evidence: Full body wash, Wheel washing, Exhaust tip polishing.

Mobile Car Wash

A mobile car wash is the routine-cleaning option in the documented menu: a full-body wash brought to the agreed location, with wheel washing available when it is included in the appointment.

Menu evidence: Full body wash, Wheel washing.

Headlight Restoration & Polishing

Headlight polishing is a focused appearance service for suitable exterior lens surfaces; it is assessed separately from a full wash or complete detail.

Menu evidence: Headlight polishing.
